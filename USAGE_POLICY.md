# Intended use and service policy

The maintained application is intended for independent, noncommercial,
nongambling visual observation research. Record visible equine posture, body
appearance, hindlimbs, sweat and related features, with uncertainty and evidence.
Do not turn observations into race predictions, odds, expected monetary value,
selection instructions, stakes or purchases. Do not supply SIWC results to a
real-money gambling system automatically or manually. No such route is implemented.

Obtain the rights needed to read and, separately, transmit each image to an
external AI service. A subscription or ability to view media does not itself
establish permission to redistribute it or send it elsewhere. The source includes
only newly authored synthetic examples; users' media is never part of a release.

Use only this app's formal SIWC connection. Do not copy credentials from other
software, share accounts or tokens, pool usage, rotate identities to evade limits,
or expose a generic proxy. Do not charge for access to the SIWC plan-use feature.
This application has no subscription or credit-selling feature.

Before live use, review the current [SIWC terms](https://openai.com/policies/sign-in-with-chatgpt-terms/)
and [OpenAI usage policies](https://openai.com/policies/usage-policies/).
Account access, model availability, app caps and credits remain separate gates.
The current offline tests do not certify any user's actual entitlement.

MIT governs the newly authored software copyright. The intended-use statement
does not add a noncommercial restriction to MIT or label a restricted license
as open source. OpenAI service use has its own terms, regardless of what the
software license permits. The project does not license third-party images,
private research, external service software, or OpenAI branding.
